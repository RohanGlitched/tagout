Lidl US Recalls Gelatelli Coffee & Vanilla Gelato Due to Possible Health Risk
Company Announcement Date: October 16, 2026
FDA Publish Date: October 17, 2026
Company Name: Lidl US
Brand Name: Gelatelli
Product Description: Coffee & Vanilla Gelato with Coffee Sauce & Cookie Pieces
Lidl US is recalling one lot of Gelatelli Coffee & Vanilla Gelato with Coffee Sauce & Cookie Pieces 16 FL OZ (1 PT) UPC 4 0564897 22458 Lot: 085261 Best By MAR/26/2028 because it has the potential to be contaminated with Listeria monocytogenes.
The recalled product can be identified as follows: Gelatelli Coffee & Vanilla Gelato with Coffee Sauce & Cookie Pieces 16 FL OZ (1 PT) UPC 4 0564897 22458 Lot: 085261 Best By MAR/26/2028, which can be found on the bottom of the container.
