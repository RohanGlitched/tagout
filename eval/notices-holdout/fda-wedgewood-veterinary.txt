Wedgewood Pharmacy Issues Recall of Certain Compounded Veterinary Medications Within Expiry Due to Potential Potency Issues
Company Announcement Date: October 21, 2026
FDA Publish Date: October 21, 2026
Product Type: Animal & Veterinary, Drugs
Company Name: Wedgewood Pharmacy, LLC.
Product Description: Compounded veterinary medications
SWEDESBORO, N.J., (October 21, 2026) - Wedgewood Pharmacy LLC ("Wedgewood") is recalling certain compounded veterinary medications within expiry at the provider/customer level due to inconsistencies in the reliability of certain analytical testing data generated by their laboratory, potentially resulting in subpotent and/or superpotent products. The affected products were shipped between February 18, 2026 and October 6, 2026 to veterinary practices, pharmacies, and other customers nationwide in the U.S. A complete list of affected products (including powders, pastes, oral suspensions, injectables, ophthalmic ointments and solutions, ear solutions and suspensions, topical sprays, and gels), lot numbers, and expiration dates are available at www.wedgewood.com/recall-lookup/. The products involved are intended for companion, equine, exotic, zoo, wildlife, and aquatic animals.
Animal owners who have received affected product should stop use, retain the product, and contact Wedgewood's recall services by phone at 866-562-5931.
