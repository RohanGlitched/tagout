WinCo Foods LLC Issues Allergy Alert on Undeclared Sesame in Hoagie Rolls
Company Announcement Date: October 22, 2026
FDA Publish Date: October 22, 2026
Company Name: WinCo Foods LLC
Brand Name: WinCo Foods
Product Description: Hoagie Rolls
October 22, 2026 - WinCo Foods of Boise, ID is recalling the following WinCo Foods Brand Hoagie Rolls due to undeclared sesame.
WinCo Foods Hoagie Rolls with UPC 2-70552-00649-2, with a net weight of 16oz(1LB) 454g, with Best By Dates of 9/10/2026 - 11/3/2026. The best by date will be formatted as "Best if used by SEP 9" printed on the bag.
