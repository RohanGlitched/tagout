026-2026 HIGH - CLASS I PROCESSING DEFECT
Kayem Foods Inc. Recalls Meat and Poultry Products Due to Possible Temperature Abuse
WASHINGTON, Oct. 17, 2026 - Kayem Foods Inc., a Chelsea, Mass. establishment, is recalling approximately 1,584,361 pounds of ready-to-eat (RTE) meat and poultry products because they may have experienced temperature abuse during storage.
The RTE meat and poultry products items were produced approximately March 31, 2026, to Oct. 7, 2026.
The products subject to recall bear establishment numbers "EST. M/P 7839," "EST. P 20935A" and "EST. M 9520" inside the USDA mark of inspection. These items were shipped to retail and foodservice locations Nationwide and Hong Kong.
