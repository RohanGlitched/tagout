FDA Advises Not to Eat, Sell, or Serve Cold Smoked Sprats Sold by Haifa Smoked Fish, Inc.
Do not eat, sell, or serve any cold smoked sprats sold by Haifa Smoked Fish, Inc. due to potential Clostridium botulinum contamination.

Additional Product Images
Product:
All lots of cold smoked sprats sold by D&M Smoked Fish doing-business-as (DBA) Haifa Smoked Fish, Inc. Product is sold in bulk cardboard boxes and shipped refrigerated. It may be frozen up to one year. This product may have been sold at restaurants and grocery store seafood counters with or without a label. Consumers who are unsure whether they have purchased cold smoked sprats from Haifa Smoked Fish, Inc. should throw them away or contact their place of purchase.
Symptoms of botulism:
Nausea, vomiting, fatigue or weakness, blurred or double vision, difficulty speaking or change in voice, difficulty swallowing, descending paralysis starting with the face, respiratory distress, dry mouth, and constipation. In severe cases, people will die without intensive care. People experiencing these problems should seek immediate medical attention.
Stores affected:
Cold smoked sprats were sold to retailers, restaurants, wholesalers, and food service customers in Connecticut, New Jersey, New York, and Pennsylvania. Product may have been distributed further, reaching additional states.
Recommendation:
Current Update
October 23, 2026
The FDA is warning consumers, retailers, wholesalers, restaurants, and food service customers not to eat, sell, or serve any cold smoked sprats sold by D&M Smoked Fish Inc. (DBA) Haifa Smoked Fish, Inc. Cold smoked sprats were produced without implementing required controls for Clostridium botulinum and are a potential health risk to consumers.
FDA initiated an inspection at D&M Smoked Fish Inc., of Jamacia, NY, and the inspection revealed that the firm was producing cold-smoked sprats without required food safety controls to prevent Clostridium botulinum growth and toxin formation. FDA has recommended D&M Smoked Fish Inc. recall all cold smoked sprats currently on the market, but the firm has declined. FDA is issuing this alert to warn consumers about the potential risk for botulism if they consume cold smoked sprats sold by Haifa Smoked Fish, Inc.
Food contaminated with Clostridium botulinum toxin can look, taste or smell normal, but still make you sick. Do not eat cold smoked sprats from Haifa Smoked Fish, Inc. Retailers, restaurants, wholesalers, and food service customers should not sell or serve cold smoked sprats sold by Haifa Smoked Fish, Inc. If you are unsure if your cold smoked sprats are from Haifa Smoked Fish, Inc., contact your place of purchase or supplier.   
FDA will update this alert with additional information, as it becomes available.
