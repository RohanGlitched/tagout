Pan Pepin Inc. Issues Allergy Alert of Para Bien Al Grano Due to Undeclared Sesame

Summary
Company Announcement
Pan Pepin Inc. of Bayamón, Puerto Rico is recalling certain lots of Para Bien Al Grano because the product contain sesame.
People who have an allergy or severe sensitivity to sesame may risk a serious or life- threatening allergic reaction if they consume these products.
The product was distributed throughout Puerto Rico.
